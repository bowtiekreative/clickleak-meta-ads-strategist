# Creative Playbook

## Angles, in the order to test them
1. **Testimonial**: a real customer on camera or quoted by name.
2. **Personal story**: a true, specific moment from the founder or customer.
3. **Problem call-out**: the buyer's pain in their own words.
4. **Contrast**: two kinds of X, and which one you are.
5. **Education**: a checklist, framework or mistake to avoid.
6. **Self-introduction / pitch**: "Hi, I'm..." Usually the weakest opener. Test it last.

Angle often matters more than format. A testimonial video and a self-intro video are not the same test.

## Hooks (first 3 seconds or first line)
- Call out the buyer: "[Role]: you did [the usual thing]. Nothing changed."
- Hidden cost: "You're paying for [X]. Then [Y] wastes it."
- Contrast: "Two kinds of [offer]. One ends in [A]. The other ends in [B]."
- Proof first: the testimonial line, then the name.
- Real number: a count or result the user can prove.
- Confession: one true sentence most people would not say out loud.

## Video (15 to 45 seconds)
| Time | Job |
|---|---|
| 0 to 3 s | Hook + name or brand on screen + burned-in captions |
| 3 to 10 s | Problem in the buyer's words |
| 10 to 25 s | Proof |
| 25 to 35 s | What changes, in one line |
| Last 5 s | One CTA that matches the landing page |

Most viewers watch with sound off. Read the metrics: hook rate (3 s views / impressions), 25 / 50 / 100% retention, ThruPlays, average watch time. Clicks are mostly decided early, so fix the opening before the ending.

## Static
- Real photo of the person or product
- One line of text, large, high contrast
- No paragraphs on the image

## Copy fields
- Primary text: short version under about 125 characters before "See more", plus a long story version
- Headline: 1 to 25 characters
- CTA button that matches the page (BOOK_NOW, SIGN_UP, LEARN_MORE, GET_QUOTE, CONTACT_US, DONATE_NOW)

## Rules
- Use proof verbatim. Never invent a testimonial, review, number or client.
- Page headline repeats the ad promise.
- One ad, one promise, one action.
